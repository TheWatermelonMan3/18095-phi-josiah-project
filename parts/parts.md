# Parts

Here are the files I used to build the robot:
 * **Soda Rover 1 of 2.stl** contains the robot frame, rear sliders, rack and pinion, camera holder (not used in final version), and stepper motor holder.
 * **Soda Rover 2 of 2.stl** contains the wheels (not used in final version) two stepper motor holders, and a roof (not used in final version).
 * **Soda Rover Final Parts.stl** contains two stepper motor holders and two wheels.
 * **sodarovermissingparts.stl** contains a better camera holder and three better stepper motor holders.
 * **SodaRoverRoof.dxf** is a laser cut for the roof of the robot.
 * **two wheel cut with holes fixed.dxf** is a laser cut for the wheels of the robot.
